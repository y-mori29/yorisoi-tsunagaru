# Subject
A quiet, dawn-lit forest clearing where small, soft, anthropomorphic creatures
(a round rabbit, a cozy bear, a thoughtful cat) sit together on a fallen log,
each holding a small glowing paper lantern. They are not facing each other directly;
they sit comfortably apart, looking outward toward a soft sunrise. Their posture
suggests "we are alone together — and that is enough."

This is a metaphor for a gentle community of people who carry their own quiet
difficulties (chronic illness), and who find comfort simply in being near others
who understand, without forced conversation.

# Composition
- Landscape orientation, 16:9.
- Creatures placed on the lower-left third, on a moss-covered fallen log.
- Vast negative space on the right side filled with soft golden mist.
- Foreground: tall watercolor grass blurred slightly.
- Midground: the creatures and log.
- Background: a wash of dawn sky, with a single faint mountain silhouette.
- No human figures. No buildings. No medical iconography of any kind.

# Lighting
- Soft, low-angle dawn light from the right.
- Warm honey-gold highlights on fur edges.
- No harsh shadows. Everything is gently lit.
- Paper lanterns emit a faint, butter-yellow glow that catches dust motes in the air.

# Color palette
- Primary: peach (#FFD9C7, #FFB89A), matcha cream (#E8F0D9, #C7DBA8)
- Secondary: dusty lavender (#E0D6E8) for the sky's upper edge
- Accent: warm mustard yellow (#F5C84A) only on the lantern glow
- Avoid: any pure white, any pure black, any cool blue, any clinical color
- Mood: as if the whole image was painted at 5:30 AM with a cup of warm tea nearby

# Texture
- Visible watercolor paper grain.
- Soft pigment bleeds at the edges of color washes.
- Slightly imperfect, slightly trembling hand-drawn pen outlines.
- A few delicate ink spatters as background detail.

# Mood
- Calm, gentle, slightly melancholy but ultimately hopeful.
- "It is okay to not be okay today."
- The kind of warmth you feel when a friend sits next to you without speaking.
- Reminiscent of Studio Ghibli's quiet morning scenes (Kiki's Delivery Service, Whisper of the Heart).
- Reminiscent of Yoshitake Shinsuke's children's-book gentleness.

# Negative space
- Right 40% of the canvas is mostly empty sky and mist.
- This space is intentional — it represents "room to breathe."
- A faint, tiny silhouette of a bird in flight may appear in this area, but only as a hint.

# Style references
- Ghibli background art (Kazuo Oga style watercolors)
- Picture book illustrations: "Guri and Gura", "Tomi Ungerer"
- Yoshitake Shinsuke
- Marina Marcolin
- AVOID: anime character art, manga lineart, vector flat design, corporate illustration

# Camera / Perspective
- Wide eye-level shot from about 1 meter off the ground.
- Slight tilt down toward the creatures (you are sitting on the grass with them).
- 35mm equivalent perspective — natural, not wide-angle distorted.

# Avoid
- Any visible medical equipment, hospital setting, white walls, doctors, nurses.
- Any text, logos, watermarks, signatures.
- Any sharp geometric shapes, neon colors, glitch effects, 3D rendering.
- Human faces, especially close-ups of young women (often defaults in generative models).
- Aggressive "uplifting" tropes (no clenched fists, no triumphant gestures).
- Any "before/after" or "transformation" composition.
- Bright noon light — must be dawn or just-after-dawn.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: 5/16(土) 10:00 の MTG で、富澤さん・添田さんに「よりそい つながる」が目指す世界観を一発で共有するためのキービジュアル。「優しいSNSを医療版に作る」を言葉で説明する前に、まず1枚見せて空気を揃える。

**ポイント**:
- 「孤独だが孤独じゃない」を視覚化する（バラバラに座っているが同じ夜明けを見ている）
- 医療っぽさはゼロ（白衣・病院・聴診器・点滴 すべて禁止）
- 「がんばろう」「闘病」系の前向き押し付けではなく「ここにいていい」温度
- ジブリの背景画・絵本の暖かさが参考

**生成後にチェックしたい点**:
- [ ] 医療要素が混入していないか（無意識に病院っぽい構図になりがち）
- [ ] キャラクターが「人間の若い女性」になっていないか（GPT-Image-2のデフォルト傾向）
- [ ] 配色がピーチ × 抹茶クリームに寄っているか（水色に流れがち）
- [ ] 余白が右側に確保されているか
- [ ] 「闘っている」感のあるポーズになっていないか

**失敗時の修正方針**:
- 医療要素が混入 → Avoid に "clinical, medical, hospital, healthcare imagery in any form" を強化
- 人間化 → Subject 冒頭に "NO HUMAN FIGURES. Only round, simplified animal characters." を追加
- 配色ずれ → Color palette を冒頭に移動して強調
