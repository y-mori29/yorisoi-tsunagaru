# Subject
A single, slim, elegant, long-tailed cat character named "Sora."
He is in a walking pose, mid-stride, one front paw lifted slightly,
his long curling tail trailing softly behind him, gently curved.
His head is turned to look back at the viewer over his shoulder,
as if inviting them to follow on a small walk.

His ears are tall and slightly curved. His eyes are narrow, sleepy,
half-closed in a calm "going-on-a-walk" expression — NOT wide alert eyes,
NOT predatory.

Sora's fur is a soft matcha-cream color (#E8F0D9 → #C7DBA8 gradient),
with slightly darker cream on his ears, tail tip, and paws. His belly
and chest are paler cream-white.

This character represents "the gentle nudge to go explore quietly,
without obligation."

# Composition
- Centered single character, walking pose, mostly in profile but with
  head turned to viewer (3/4 angle).
- Character occupies the central 60-70% of the canvas, with the long
  tail extending into the background.
- A few faint watercolor leaves on the ground beneath him (suggesting
  a path), but very soft, almost ghostly.
- Background: pure transparent ideally, or very soft matcha-tinted
  cream wash.

# Lighting
- Soft, slightly dappled light (as if walking through tree shade).
- Warm highlights on the back, top of head, and tip of tail.
- No harsh shadows.

# Color palette
- Body: soft matcha-cream (#E8F0D9, #C7DBA8)
- Belly/chest: pale cream (#FBF5E8)
- Tail tip and inner ears: slightly darker matcha (#9BB87A)
- Eye color: warm amber (#C4944F), narrow half-closed
- Nose and paw pads: dusty rose (#C49A8E)
- Faint leaves: matcha green (#C7DBA8) at 50% opacity

# Texture
- Watercolor paper grain in the background.
- Fine pencil/ink outlines in warm brown (#6B4F3F).
- Soft fur indicated by gentle directional line marks.
- A few faint watercolor splashes near the ground for atmosphere.

# Mood
- Calm, quietly curious, gentle.
- "Come, walk with me. We don't have to go anywhere in particular."
- The kind of cat that doesn't pressure you, just leads softly.
- Reminiscent of the cat from Studio Ghibli's Whisper of the Heart
  (Baron's quieter cousin) and Hayao Miyazaki's gentle animal
  character designs.

# Negative space
- Generous margins (15% all sides) for UI cropping.
- The tail can extend into the right side of the canvas, giving the
  composition a sense of "going somewhere."

# Style references
- Studio Ghibli quieter cat characters
- Picture book illustrations: Cat from Komako Sakai books,
  Edward Gorey's gentler cats (less spooky), Kazuo Iwamura
- AVOID: anime cat girl tropes, manga, vector flat design,
  corporate mascot, realistic 3D cat, alert/predatory cat poses

# Camera / Perspective
- Profile to 3/4 angle, eye-level with the cat (camera low).
- Walking direction: from left to right, with head turned back to viewer.

# Avoid
- Wide alert eyes, predatory crouching, hunting pose.
- Big anime eyes, sparkly highlights.
- Any clothing, accessories beyond the natural cat form.
- Any human gestures or expressions.
- Any medical or hospital imagery.
- Text, logos, watermarks.
- Photorealism, 3D rendering.
- Multiple cats — ONLY ONE cat.
- Sexualization of any kind.

- 長いしっぽが画面右側にカーブして抜けると動きが出る。
