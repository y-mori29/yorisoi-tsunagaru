# Subject
A close-up shot of an old wooden bookshelf or shelf section, with:
- 3-4 hardcover books standing upright, spines facing the viewer
  (cloth-bound, with no readable text on the spines — just abstract muted
  colored bands)
- one book lying horizontally on top
- a small ceramic cup (empty, no liquid) placed in front of the books
- a single small dried plant in a tiny earth-toned pot
- soft golden hour light filtering through (perhaps from a window
  out of frame to the right)

No people. No screens. No modern brand elements. A quiet, lived-in shelf.

# Composition
- Vertical 4:5 (1024 × 1280).
- Shelf occupies the middle 60% of the frame.
- Books on the left, cup and pot on the right.
- Soft golden light enters from the right side.
- Background is the wall behind the shelf, slightly out of focus.

# Lighting
- Golden hour light (warm orange-amber) from the right.
- Long, soft shadows cast to the left of each object.
- A few specks of dust visible in the light beam (very subtle).

# Color palette
- Shelf wood: warm walnut (#7E5A40, #5A3F2A)
- Book spines: muted earth tones — moss green (#A2AB7D),
  dusty terracotta (#A47556), warm cream (#DCD2C0), faded plum (#8E7E92)
- Ceramic cup: cream with terracotta rim (#EFDCC9 / #C99F7B)
- Dried plant: faded sage (#B0B89A)
- Plant pot: warm earth-brown ceramic (#A88A6E)
- Golden hour light: warm honey (#D9B57E, #E8C68E)
- Background wall: shadowed cream (#D6C8B2)

# Texture
- Visible wood grain on the shelf, slightly worn.
- Cloth-bound book covers with visible weave texture.
- Hand-thrown ceramic feel on the cup and pot.
- Slight dust on the shelf surface, very subtle.

# Mood
- **End of the day. Quiet contemplation. Self time.**
- Like a private library corner at the end of a long day.
- Like a moment in a Yasujirō Ozu film.

# Negative space
- Upper area (above the shelf): warm shadowed wall.
- Right side: where the golden light enters — luminous and soft.

# Style references
- Naoya Hatakeyama photography
- Kinfolk magazine
- Late afternoon scenes from Ozu films
- AVOID: instagram bookstagram aesthetic, color-coded book displays,
  modern minimalist library photos, stock images

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the shelf (camera at shelf height).
- Slight 3/4 angle from the left.
- Lens: 50mm, shallow depth of field on the books.

# Avoid
- Any people, hands, or human silhouettes.
- Readable text on book spines (must be blurred or abstract).
- Modern devices, electronics.
- Multiple cups (only one).
- Saturated colors, neon highlights.
- Heavy shadows, dramatic noir lighting.
- Steam from the cup (should be empty).
- Text, logos, watermarks.
