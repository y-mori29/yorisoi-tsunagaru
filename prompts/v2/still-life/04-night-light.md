# Subject
A small bedside or desk corner at night. The scene includes:
- a small warm-glow lamp (the lamp itself with a cream linen shade,
  emitting a soft warm light)
- an open notebook with a single pen resting on it (pages blank,
  no readable text)
- a small ceramic mug (empty, no liquid, no steam)
- the lamp casts a warm circle of light that softly illuminates these objects

No people. No screens. No medical items.

# Composition
- Square 1:1.
- The lamp at upper-left, its shade catching the light.
- The notebook spans the center-lower area.
- The mug at lower-right.
- The rest of the frame is gentle darkness — but never pitch black.
  More like "twilight indoor" — warm shadows with detail still visible.

# Lighting
- Single warm light source from the lamp.
- Light falls in a soft circle on the notebook and mug.
- Surrounding shadows are warm dark beige (NOT black, NOT cold).
- No additional light sources.

# Color palette
- Lamp glow center: warm honey-yellow (#E8C68E, #D9B57E)
- Lamp shade: cream linen (#EFDCC9)
- Notebook pages (illuminated): warm cream (#EFDCC9)
- Notebook pages (in shadow): muted dusty beige (#A88A6E)
- Mug: warm earth-tone ceramic (#A47556)
- Pen: dark sepia wood (#5A3F2A)
- Background shadow: warm deep beige (#3E342A) — NEVER pure black
- Wall texture: very faint warm gray-brown (#5A4E40)

# Texture
- Hand-thrown ceramic feel on the mug.
- Linen weave visible on the lamp shade.
- Paper grain on the notebook.
- Soft cinematic film grain across the whole image (very subtle).

# Mood
- **Late evening. Quiet end of the day. Solitude with comfort.**
- Like a corner of a small inn room.
- The light is warm enough that you feel taken care of.
- Not lonely. Not melancholy. Quietly present.

# Negative space
- Upper-right is mostly warm shadow — restful.
- The light circle is intentional and central.

# Style references
- Andrew Wyeth's quiet evening interiors
- Vermeer (the small intimate scenes, NOT the famous portraits)
- Naomi Kawase film stills (slow evening moments)
- AVOID: noir film aesthetic (too high contrast),
  Christmas/holiday warm-cozy stock images (too sweet),
  Instagram "cozy night in" filter aesthetic

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the table/desk surface.
- Slight 3/4 angle from the right.
- Lens: 50mm, shallow depth of field on the notebook.

# Avoid
- Any people, hands, or silhouettes.
- Modern devices: phones, laptops, charging cables.
- Medical items.
- Pure black backgrounds (must be warm dark beige).
- Bright highlights, dramatic light rays.
- Text on the notebook (must be blank).
- Multiple light sources (only the lamp).
- Steam, smoke, mist.
