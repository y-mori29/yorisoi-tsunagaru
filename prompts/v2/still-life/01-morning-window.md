# Subject
A quiet morning still life, shot or illustrated like an editorial magazine
feature on slow Japanese living.

The scene: a wooden window sill at dawn, with a single small ceramic cup
of tea (warm earth-tone glaze, no steam shown), beside a small folded linen
cloth, and a single green leaf (just one) on the wood. Outside the window
(slightly out of focus), a hint of a cream curtain catching the first light,
and a faint silhouette of trees in the distance.

No people. No faces. No medical or hospital references. Just a quiet
moment that says: "Here is a place where you can exhale."

# Composition
- Vertical 4:5 aspect ratio (1024 × 1280).
- Window sill takes the lower 60% of the frame.
- The cup is positioned at the lower-left third (rule of thirds).
- The linen cloth is folded behind the cup, slightly to the right.
- A single small green leaf is on the wood in front of the cup.
- Upper 40% is the soft light from the window — diffused, calming.
- Slight depth-of-field blur on the background (curtain/trees).

# Lighting
- Early morning light from upper-left.
- Warm, golden-honey tones from the light, fading to soft beige in shadows.
- No harsh contrast. Everything is gently illuminated.
- The cup catches a small soft highlight on its rim.

# Color palette
- Wood: warm honey-brown (#A47556, #7E5A40)
- Cup: muted terracotta glaze (#C99F7B), darker rim (#A47556)
- Cloth: cream linen (#EFDCC9)
- Leaf: muted moss green (#A2AB7D)
- Background light: warm cream (#FAF6EE)
- Faint outline of trees: soft sage (#C0C6A1)
- **Avoid**: any saturated colors, bright pinks, neon, pure white, deep black

# Texture
- Photographic-illustration quality. Realistic but slightly painterly.
- Visible wood grain on the sill.
- Soft fabric texture on the linen.
- The cup has a hand-thrown ceramic feel — slight imperfections.
- Watercolor-like wash for the background light.

# Mood
- **Slow morning. Permission to rest. Adult dignity.**
- Like a still from a Naomi Kawase film, or a Hayao Miyazaki morning scene
  without the characters.
- The kind of image that makes someone exhale when they see it.
- Calm, grounded, respectful.

# Negative space
- The upper 40% is intentional negative space (light).
- The right side has breathing room — no clutter.
- The composition should feel restful, not busy.

# Style references
- Naoya Hatakeyama photography
- Hiroshi Sugimoto's quiet stillness
- Magazine layouts: Kinfolk, Brutus's Japan special issues
- Studio Ghibli backgrounds (especially the slow domestic moments)
- AVOID: stock photography, Instagram-aesthetic, food blog style,
  high-saturation product photography

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the window sill (camera at sill height).
- Slight 3/4 angle from the right.
- Lens: 50mm equivalent, natural perspective.
- Depth of field: shallow but not extreme — the cup and cloth are sharp,
  the background is softly blurred.

# Avoid
- Any people, faces, hands, or human silhouettes.
- Any medical equipment, hospital props, medicine bottles, syringes.
- Steam from the cup (too dramatic).
- Sharp focus on the background trees (should be soft).
- Bright sunlight, strong shadows, dramatic lighting.
- Multiple cups, multiple objects (keep it minimal — one of each).
- Any text, logos, brand names visible on the cup or cloth.
- Saturated colors or neon highlights.
- Cute or whimsical elements (no flowers in vases, no decorative birds).
- Children-book illustration style.
