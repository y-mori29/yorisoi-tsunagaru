# Subject
A quiet pathway through a small grove of trees, viewed from the start of
the path. The path is narrow, gently curving, and made of natural dirt
or worn stone. Trees on both sides — modest, not towering.
Soft early morning or late afternoon light filters through the leaves.

No people. No road signs. No urban infrastructure. Just the quiet path
inviting the viewer.

# Composition
- Horizontal 16:9 (1280 × 720).
- The path begins at the lower-center and curves slightly to the right
  before disappearing into the trees.
- Trees frame the path on both sides (taller on the left).
- Sky visible in the upper third, soft and pale.
- Some small undergrowth and fallen leaves on the path edges.

# Lighting
- Soft golden hour (either dawn or just before sunset, but very gentle).
- Light filters through leaves creating a few soft warm spots on the path.
- No dramatic god-rays or harsh contrast.
- The whole scene is bathed in warm diffused light.

# Color palette
- Path: warm earth (#A88A6E, #7E5A40)
- Trees / foliage: muted moss-sage (#A2AB7D, #B5BCA0), some warm autumn
  tinges (#C99F7B) if season-appropriate
- Sky: pale cream-peach (#EFDCC9, #F3E8D8)
- Fallen leaves: muted terracotta and moss (#A47556, #9BA481)
- Tree trunks: warm brown (#7E5A40, #5A3F2A)

# Texture
- Visible texture on the path (worn stones or natural dirt with small
  pebbles).
- Soft leaf texture on trees, NOT hyper-detailed.
- Slight watercolor wash effect on the whole image (NOT photoreal).
- Cinematic film grain (very subtle).

# Mood
- **Invitation without pressure. A walk you can take, or not.**
- Like a small forest path you might find behind a temple in Kyoto, or
  on the edge of a quiet farming village in Shikoku.
- Calm, grounded, slightly nostalgic.

# Negative space
- The center of the path (where it curves and disappears) is a focal
  vanishing point — let it draw the eye.
- Upper third (sky) is mostly negative space.

# Style references
- Naoya Hatakeyama landscape photography
- Hiroshi Sugimoto's quiet landscapes
- Hayao Miyazaki's pastoral backgrounds (the calm ones, not adventure scenes)
- Yasujirō Ozu countryside framing
- AVOID: dramatic landscape photography (Ansel Adams), postcard tourist
  views, overly saturated Instagram nature filters, fantasy concept art

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level (camera at standing-walking height).
- The viewer is at the start of the path.
- Lens: 35mm equivalent, natural perspective with mild depth of field.

# Avoid
- Any people, footprints, hand-held objects.
- Road signs, benches, lampposts, fences (anything man-made urban).
- Bright sunlight, dramatic shadows.
- Snow, rain, weather drama.
- Tourist landmarks (no temple gates, no famous bridges).
- Saturated foliage colors (no bright autumn red, no vivid green).
- Cars, bicycles, animals.
- Text, watermarks.

**ポイント**:
- 派手な紅葉 / 桜 / 観光地は禁止。
- ドラマチックな光線は禁止（柔らかい朝or夕方）。
- 人や動物・標識など現代の存在を入れない。
