# Subject

A hand-drawn **editorial line illustration** showing a simple folded letter
(envelope-style fold) with one small wax seal in the center, lying gently
on a wooden surface. Beside the letter, a single sprig of dried lavender.
Nothing else in the scene.

**The wordless message**: "A quiet letter has arrived for you. No urgency."

# Composition

- 4:3 horizontal aspect (1024 × 768)
- Letter slightly off-center (left of center), folded envelope shape, ~45% of width
- Lavender sprig diagonal to the right of the letter, gently overlapping
- Suggested wooden surface below: just a few minimal grain lines, no detailed planks
- Generous breathing room around (15%+ margin)

# Color palette

- Line: warm sepia #7E5A40 (1.5pt stroke, hand-drawn imperfection)
- Letter (paper): very pale cream #F3EDE2 with subtle wash
- Wax seal: small terra #A47556 dot in center
- Lavender stem: thin sepia line
- Lavender flowers: faint plum #D8CFD9 dabs (suggesting buds, not detailed)
- Lavender leaves: faint moss #DCE0C8
- Wood surface: warm honey #C99F7B (very light wash, suggested only)
- Background: **TRANSPARENT** ideal, otherwise cream paper #FAF6EE

# Texture

- Hand-drawn ink outline (slightly imperfect, single confident stroke)
- Very subtle watercolor wash inside letter and lavender
- Paper grain visible
- NO drop shadows

# Style references

- 暮しの手帖 cover illustration
- MUJI catalog still-life series
- Yumi Kitagishi quiet objects
- Quentin Blake restrained line drawing
- AVOID: romantic letter aesthetic (no hearts, no red wax), generic stock
  illustration, photo, 3D

# Avoid

- NO person, NO hands holding the letter
- NO ribbons (just the lavender sprig, no decorative ribbons)
- NO hearts, NO romantic colors (the wax seal is small terra, not red)
- NO multiple letters
- NO opened/unfolded letter showing words inside
- NO text, NO writing visible on the letter
- NO photo realism, NO 3D shading
- NO bright colors
