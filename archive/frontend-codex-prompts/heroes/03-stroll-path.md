# Subject

A hand-drawn **editorial line illustration** showing a small winding path
that gently disappears into a few simplified trees in the distance. The
trees are minimal (3–5 trees total), leaves suggested as soft cloud-like
shapes rather than drawn in detail. The path is suggested with two parallel
gentle curves. Far in the background (small, at the horizon), the
silhouette of a tiny house with a barely-visible warm light in its window.

**The wordless message**: "A path is here, leading somewhere quiet."

# Composition

- 4:3 horizontal aspect (1024 × 768)
- Path enters from bottom-center, gently curving away into mid-ground
- 3–5 simplified trees flanking the path (cloud-like canopies)
- Tiny house in upper third, very small (5–8% of width), suggesting distance
- Tiny warm-light glow in the house window (terra-50 hint, very subtle)
- Sky / upper portion: empty paper / very pale cream

# Color palette

- Line: warm sepia #7E5A40 (1.5pt stroke, hand-drawn imperfection)
- Path: very pale cream #F3EDE2 wash inside the two curves
- Tree trunks: thin sepia lines
- Tree canopies: faint moss #DCE0C8 (cloud-like soft wash)
- House: small sepia outline, tiny terra-50 (#F8EFE7) dot for window light
- Background: **TRANSPARENT** ideal, otherwise cream paper #FAF6EE

# Texture

- Hand-drawn ink outline (gently trembling)
- Very subtle watercolor wash for path and tree canopies
- Paper grain visible
- NO drop shadows, NO god-rays, NO dramatic light

# Style references

- 暮しの手帖 cover illustration
- Studio Ghibli quiet background sketches (Totoro path scenes, but more restrained)
- Yoshitake Shinsuke adult-tone landscape
- Quentin Blake landscape line drawings
- AVOID: Adobe Stock landscape illustration, fantasy game backgrounds,
  photo, 3D

# Avoid

- NO people, NO animals on the path
- NO detailed leaves (must remain suggestion-level — cloud-like shapes)
- NO dramatic light beams, NO god-rays
- NO sun visible
- NO mountains in the background (just trees + tiny house)
- NO photo, NO 3D rendering
- NO bright saturated colors (autumn red, neon green)
- NO multiple houses or village (just ONE tiny house)
- NO text, NO signs along the path
