# Subject

A hand-drawn **editorial line illustration** showing a simple domestic
scene: a soft floor cushion (zabuton) placed on a wooden floor, with a
small low table beside it holding one ceramic teacup, and a small closed
book on the floor.

**The wordless message of this scene**: "Your seat has been prepared.
Take your time. Stay as long or as briefly as you want."

NO person. NO face. NO hand. NO foot. NO body part. NO window. NO landscape.

# Composition

- 4:5 vertical aspect (1024 × 1280)
- Lower 2/3 contains the scene; upper 1/3 is breathing room (empty / very faint paper)
- Center-left: a single square zabuton (floor cushion) with rounded corners,
  soft volume suggested by subtle wash inside
- Right of cushion: a small low table (chabudai style, two short legs visible),
  warm honey wood tone
- On the table: ONE simple ceramic teacup, no liquid shown, no steam, no decoration
- Right of cushion on the floor: a small closed book lying flat
- Suggested wooden floor: just a few subtle grain lines, no detailed planks
- Camera: slightly elevated angle looking down gently

# Color palette

- Line: warm sepia #7E5A40 (1.5pt stroke, hand-drawn imperfection, round caps)
- Cushion wash: cream #F3EDE2 + faint terra #EFDCC9
- Wood table: warm honey #C99F7B (very light wash)
- Teacup wash: faint plum #D8CFD9
- Book wash: faint moss #DCE0C8
- Background: **TRANSPARENT** ideal, otherwise cream paper #FAF6EE with very subtle paper grain

# Texture

- Hand-drawn ink outline (slightly trembling, not vector-perfect)
- Very subtle watercolor wash inside each object — minimal layering
- Paper grain visible
- NO drop shadows, NO 3D shading

# Style references

- 暮しの手帖 magazine cover illustrations
- MUJI Japanese catalog interior / still-life illustrations
- Yumi Kitagishi quiet still-life
- Yoshitake Shinsuke adult-tone (NOT children's book mode)
- Quentin Blake restrained line work
- Naoko Stoop quiet domestic scenes
- AVOID: Notion-style corporate editorial illustrations, vector flat illustration,
  isometric design, photo realism, 3D rendering

# Avoid

- ANY person, face, hand, foot, body part, silhouette of human
- Window, curtain, landscape outside (the scene is interior-only, but
  walls / windows are NOT drawn — suggest the floor only)
- Medical / clinical items
- Photo, 3D rendering, photorealistic shading
- Bright colors, neon, saturated pastels
- Multiple cushions or chairs (only ONE cushion)
- Steam from teacup, flowers, decorations on book
- Text, logos, watermarks
