# Subject

A single small **hedgehog**, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT Sonic. NOT a cartoon hedgehog. A small, contemplative round hedgehog.

Pose: sitting calmly facing slightly toward viewer (3/4). Round body with
soft spine texture on top. Small face peeking out — tiny ink-dot eye, tiny
nose, small closed mouth.

The hedgehog feels small and quiet — almost like a round stone that happens
to have a face.

# Composition

- Single hedgehog, centered, 55% of canvas height
- At least 20% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO background, NO leaves, NO apples, NO scenery

# Color palette

- Body (back/spines): warm sepia-brown (#C99F7B, #A47556) — earth-toned
- Face / belly: cream (#EFDCC9)
- Eye dot: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Nose: small sepia dot
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor wash on body
- Spine texture suggested by short repeated sepia strokes on the back/top
  (gentle, not aggressive)
- Paper grain visible
- Hand-drawn imperfect outline

# Style references

- MUJI catalog quiet animal illustrations
- 暮しの手帖 garden animals
- Quentin Blake restrained animal portraits
- AVOID: Sonic the Hedgehog (anime cartoon), Beatrix Potter Mrs. Tiggy-Winkle
  (too sweet), Sega character art

# Avoid

- Huge eyes
- Apples or leaves on the back
- Standing on hind legs
- Bright neon colors
- Multiple hedgehogs
- Text, logos
