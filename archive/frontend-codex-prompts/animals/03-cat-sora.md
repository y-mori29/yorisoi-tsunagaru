# Subject

A single small **cat** ("そら"), drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a Sanrio cat. NOT anime. NOT kawaii.

Pose: sitting calmly in a slightly turned 3/4 pose, head + upper chest visible.
Pointed triangular ears upright. Quiet, composed expression — tiny ink-dot eyes,
small closed mouth. Soft fur suggested.

The cat has the unhurried dignity of a Studio Ghibli forest animal in its
quietest moment.

# Composition

- Single cat, centered, 60% of canvas height
- At least 18% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO background, NO props

# Color palette

- Body: warm grayish-moss (#A2AB7D with sepia warmth, #DCE0C8 highlights) — muted
- Inner ears: faint cream-rose (#EFDCC9), NOT bright pink
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — small dots
- Whiskers: very faint thin sepia lines (3 each side, restrained)
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor, paper grain
- Light dry-brush for fur
- Hand-drawn imperfect line

# Style references

- MUJI catalog cat illustrations
- Hiroshi Naoyuki quiet animals
- Studio Ghibli forest scene cats (background)
- AVOID: Sanrio (Hello Kitty), Doraemon, anime catgirl, Maneki-neko

# Avoid

- Big sparkly anime eyes
- Bright pink ears or nose
- Bell collars, bow ties, accessories
- Human poses (standing, peace sign)
- Saturated colors
- Multiple cats
- Text, logos
