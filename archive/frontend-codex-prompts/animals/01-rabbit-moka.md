# Subject

A single small **rabbit** ("もか", the official mascot), drawn in a quiet,
mature, editorial illustration style. NOT a children's book character.
NOT a cartoon mascot. NOT kawaii.

Pose: sitting calmly in a slightly turned 3/4 pose, facing the viewer but
slightly to the side. Posture is composed and quiet — like a small animal
in a Japanese tea garden at dawn. The expression is **almost expressionless**:
tiny ink-dot eyes, a soft small mouth that is essentially closed. No smile.
No big eyes. No exaggerated cuteness.

The rabbit feels like a respectful, quiet presence — the kind you might see
in a literary magazine illustration or on the cover of a thoughtful Japanese
essay collection.

# Composition

- Single rabbit, centered, occupying about 60% of the canvas height
- Plenty of breathing room: at least 18% margin on all sides
- NO background scenery, NO floor, NO props
- The rabbit head + chest fits naturally inside a circular avatar slot
  (the avatar UI will crop to a circle, so corners can be empty)

# Color palette

- Body: warm cream-beige (#EFDCC9, #E0C2A5) — muted, slightly grayed
- Inner ears: pale dusty rose (#E0C2A5 with very slight pink tint, NOT bright pink)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — small dots only
- Outline: thin, hand-drawn warm sepia ink (#7E5A40, 1.5pt stroke)
- Background: **TRANSPARENT** (alpha channel, no fill)

# Texture

- Fine watercolor paper grain visible in subtle areas
- Hand-drawn ink outline: slightly imperfect (single confident pen stroke)
- Soft watercolor wash inside the outline — minimal layering
- A few delicate dry-brush marks for fur subtle texture

# Style references

- MUJI Japanese catalog illustrations
- Editorial illustrations in literary magazines (Bungeishunju, Kurashi-no-Techo)
- Hayao Miyazaki's restrained background animal sketches
- Yumi Kitagishi's quiet animal illustrations
- Quentin Blake at his most restrained
- AVOID: Sanrio, anime, Pixar 3D, Disney cute, Beatrix Potter (too sweet)

# Avoid

- Big anime eyes, sparkle highlights, exaggerated cuteness
- Cartoon proportions (big head, tiny body)
- Any clothing, accessories, props (no leaves in ears, no flowers, no hats)
- Human gestures (waving paws, peace signs, hugs)
- Smiling broadly, open mouth, tongue out
- Saturated pastels, pure white background, pure black ink
- Children's book illustration style
- Multiple rabbits — only ONE
- Medical / clinical / hospital iconography
- Text, logos, watermarks
