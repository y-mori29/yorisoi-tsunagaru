# Subject

A single small **bird** ("ふう"), drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a Twitter bird. NOT cartoon Tweety. A small, contemplative songbird.

Pose: perched in a 3/4 turned pose, head + body visible. The bird looks
quietly at the viewer with a small, almost expressionless face — a tiny
ink-dot eye on the visible side, small closed beak. Wings folded calmly.

The bird feels like a small wild bird that has decided to sit nearby —
not posing for you, just being.

# Composition

- Single bird, centered, 55% of canvas height
- At least 20% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO branch, NO leaves, NO background, NO scenery

# Color palette

- Body: warm grayish-plum (#BAACBC with sepia warmth, #D8CFD9 highlights) — muted
- Beak: small, subtle sepia #7E5A40
- Eye dot: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — single small dot
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor wash for body
- Hint of feather texture with very light dry-brush
- Paper grain visible
- Hand-drawn imperfect outline

# Style references

- MUJI catalog quiet bird illustrations
- 暮しの手帖 nature illustrations
- Yumi Kitagishi quiet bird studies
- AVOID: Twitter logo bird, cartoon Tweety, Pixar Rio characters

# Avoid

- Open beak (singing) — keep closed
- Spread wings, flying poses — keep perched and still
- Bright color feathers (no neon red, no electric blue)
- Hearts, music notes, decorations
- Multiple birds
- Branches, leaves (the bird is alone, no accessories)
- Text, logos
