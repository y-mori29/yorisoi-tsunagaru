# Subject

A single small **owl**, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a Harry Potter Hedwig. NOT cartoon hooting owl. A small, contemplative
night bird.

Pose: facing forward (subtle 3/4 turn), head + chest visible. Round body
shape. Two round eyes — but rendered as **small dots** in soft circle
suggestions (no big anime owl eyes). Small beak in the middle. Round ear
tufts may or may not appear (subtle).

The owl feels like a wise quiet observer — calm presence, no drama.

# Composition

- Single owl, centered, 60% of canvas height
- At least 18% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO branch, NO night sky, NO moon, NO background

# Color palette

- Body: muted moss-gray (#A2AB7D, #DCE0C8 highlights) — earth-toned
- Belly area: cream (#EFDCC9)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — small dots inside larger eye circles
  (the eye circle is suggested with a faint thin sepia ring, not solid)
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor wash for feathers
- Slight dry-brush texture
- Paper grain visible

# Style references

- MUJI catalog quiet bird illustrations
- 暮しの手帖 nature page owls
- Vintage Japanese woodblock prints (subtle line)
- AVOID: Harry Potter Hedwig (cartoon snowy), Hoot from animated films,
  anthropomorphic owl with hat/glasses

# Avoid

- Huge anime eyes
- Spread wings, flying pose
- Saturated brown / yellow
- Cartoon glasses, hat, professor look
- Multiple owls
- Text, logos
