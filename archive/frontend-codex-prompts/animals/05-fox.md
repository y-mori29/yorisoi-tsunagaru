# Subject

A single small **fox**, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a fairy-tale trickster fox. NOT anime kitsune with multiple tails.
A small, contemplative woodland fox.

Pose: sitting calmly in a slightly turned 3/4 pose, head + upper chest
visible. Pointed ears upright. Long pointed muzzle. Quiet, composed
expression — tiny ink-dot eyes, small closed mouth.

The fox feels respectful and quiet, like a small wild animal observing
without pressure.

# Composition

- Single fox, centered, 60% of canvas height
- At least 18% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO background, NO props, NO tail visible (just head + chest)

# Color palette

- Body: muted warm rust-orange (#C99F7B with extra warmth, NOT bright orange)
- Chest / muzzle: cream (#EFDCC9)
- Inner ears: faint cream-brown
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor wash
- Slight dry-brush for fur texture
- Paper grain visible
- Hand-drawn imperfect outline

# Style references

- MUJI catalog forest animal illustrations
- Quiet Yumi Kitagishi animal portraits
- Studio Ghibli (Mononoke's quiet forest scenes)
- AVOID: Disney's Robin Hood fox, anime nine-tail fox, Zootopia Nick

# Avoid

- Sly / sneaky expression
- Smiling, tongue out, playful pose
- Multiple tails (no kitsune iconography)
- Bright neon orange
- Accessories (no scarves, no glasses)
- Multiple foxes
- Text, logos
