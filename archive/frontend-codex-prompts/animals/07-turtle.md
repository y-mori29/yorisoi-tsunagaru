# Subject

A single small **turtle**, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle. NOT a cartoon turtle. A small,
contemplative land turtle.

Pose: head extending forward from shell, looking slightly toward viewer
in 3/4 pose. Shell visible behind. Tiny ink-dot eye, small closed mouth.
Calm, slow presence.

The turtle feels patient and slow — no rush, no urgency.

# Composition

- Single turtle, centered, 60% of canvas height
- At least 18% margin all sides
- Fits in circular avatar slot
- NO background, NO grass, NO water

# Color palette

- Shell: muted moss-green (#757F55 with #A2AB7D highlights) — earth-toned,
  with subtle hexagonal pattern hint (not detailed)
- Head & neck: warmer moss (#A2AB7D with cream highlights)
- Eye dot: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Outline: sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT**

# Texture

- Subtle watercolor wash on shell
- Very subtle hexagonal pattern (suggested, not drawn explicitly)
- Paper grain visible
- Hand-drawn imperfect outline

# Style references

- MUJI catalog nature illustrations
- 暮しの手帖 garden page turtles
- Quentin Blake animal portraits
- AVOID: TMNT, Franklin the Turtle, anime turtle characters, Yertle the Turtle

# Avoid

- Bright neon green
- Cartoon expressions (smiling, surprised)
- Standing on hind legs (humanized pose)
- Multiple turtles
- Accessories (no glasses, no bandana)
- Background scenery
- Text, logos
