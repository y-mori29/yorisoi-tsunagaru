# Subject

A single small **bear** ("ぱお"), drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial style.
NOT a children's character. NOT a cartoon. NOT kawaii.

Pose: sitting calmly in a slightly turned 3/4 pose, head + upper chest visible.
The bear looks toward the viewer with a quiet, composed expression — tiny
ink-dot eyes, small closed mouth. Round ears on top. Soft fur suggested with
minimal dry-brush.

The bear feels like a quiet companion in a quiet garden — gentle, respectful,
no exaggeration.

# Composition

- Single bear, centered, head + chest filling about 65% of canvas height
- At least 15% margin on all sides
- Fits inside a circular avatar slot
- NO background, NO props

# Color palette

- Body: warm honey-brown (#C99F7B, #E0C2A5) — muted, slightly grayed
- Inner ears: slightly darker than body, faint cream-brown
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — small dots only
- Outline: warm sepia ink #7E5A40, 1.5pt
- Background: **TRANSPARENT** (alpha channel)

# Texture

- Soft watercolor wash, paper grain visible
- Subtle dry-brush for fur texture (very restrained)
- Hand-drawn imperfect outline

# Style references

- MUJI catalog, Kurashi-no-Techo cover illustrations
- Quentin Blake restrained line work
- Yumi Kitagishi animal illustrations
- AVOID: Disney bears, Care Bears, anime mascots

# Avoid

- Big eyes, sparkle highlights, exaggerated cuteness
- Bright pink cheeks, blush marks
- Honey pots, fishing rods, any accessories
- Standing on hind legs, human poses
- Saturated colors, pure white / pure black
- Multiple bears
- Text, logos
