# Subject
A single small bear, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial illustration style.
NOT a children's book character. NOT a cartoon. NOT cuddly-cute.

The bear is sitting calmly on its bottom, with both back legs gently
relaxed forward (a comfortable resting pose). The body is round but not
chubby-cartoon round — more like the soft mass of a real young bear.
Front paws rest gently on the lap or by the sides.

The head is tilted slightly downward, the gaze is soft and looking
quietly to the lower-right (not making eye contact with the viewer).
The eyes are **tiny ink dots**, almost closed (the bear is dozing
or contemplating). The mouth is a soft simple line — no smile.

This is "the quiet friend who sits next to you without speaking."

# Composition
- Single bear, centered, occupying about 55-60% of the canvas height.
- Plenty of breathing space (20%+ margins all sides).
- No background scenery, no floor, no props.
- Background: pure transparent if possible, otherwise soft cream paper
  wash (#FAF6EE) with subtle watercolor edges at the corners.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused light from upper-left.
- Very faint warm highlight on top of the head and shoulders.
- Minimal shadow under the body to ground without weight.

# Color palette
- Body: warm cream-beige (#DCD2C0, #C9BAA4) — slightly cooler than the rabbit
- Snout area: slightly darker beige (#A89880)
- Inner ears: pale dusty rose (#D9B5A3, very low saturation)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Outline: thin sepia ink (#7E5A40, ~1.5pt)
- Background (if not transparent): cream paper (#FAF6EE)

# Texture
- Visible watercolor paper grain in the background wash.
- Hand-drawn ink outline with slight imperfection.
- Soft watercolor wash inside the outline (1-2 layers max).
- A few delicate dry-brush marks for fur direction, very restrained.

# Mood
- **Drowsy, calm, comforting, quietly present**.
- "I'm here. I won't go anywhere. We don't need to talk."
- Slightly melancholy but warm — like a friend who has come to sit
  with you on a difficult day, without trying to fix anything.

# Negative space
- 20%+ margins all sides.
- Designed for both small UI use (avatar in corner) and larger placement
  (empty-state hero in mid-screen).

# Style references
- MUJI Japanese catalog illustrations
- **Komako Sakai**'s gentle bear sketches
- Hayao Miyazaki's small forest creatures (calmest moments)
- Yumi Kitagishi's quiet animal portraits
- E.H. Shepard's original Pooh Bear (the most restrained sketches)
- **暮しの手帖** 表紙の挿絵
- AVOID: Sanrio, anime cubs, Pixar bears, Disney Winnie, realistic 3D,
  any "cuddly mascot" aesthetic

# Camera / Perspective
- Slight low angle (camera at the bear's chest level).
- Frontal with a 10-degree side tilt.
- Lens equivalent: 50mm, natural perspective.

# Avoid
- Big anime eyes, shiny highlights, sparkly effects.
- Any sharp teeth, claws emphasized, growling, aggressive pose.
- Standing pose, dancing pose, waving paws, any "performing" pose.
- Any human clothing, accessories, props (no leaves, no flowers, no hats,
  no clovers, no brooches).
- Saturated pinks, neon, pure white background, pure black ink.
- Multiple bears. ONLY ONE.
- Any medical or hospital imagery.
- Text, watermarks, signatures.
- Photorealism, 3D rendering.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: 静かに隣にいるくま「ぱお」。空状態（投稿ゼロ・通知ゼロ）の画面や、夜・休息モードでひっそり登場する役。「何もしてあげない代わりに、消えない」存在感を狙う。

**ポイント**:
- 目はほぼ閉じている（うつらうつらしている感じ）。
- 視線は viewer に合わせない（プライバシーを尊重する関係性）。
- 色は **クリームベージュ系**で、ややグレーがかった大人寄り。
- 立たない・踊らない・手を振らない（過剰な動きを排除）。

**生成後にチェックしたい点**:
- [ ] 強い顔・牙・爪が描かれていないか
- [ ] 表情が「眠そう/静か」で「ニコニコ」になっていないか
- [ ] 配色がベージュ系で、もか（うさぎ）と区別できているか
- [ ] 装飾が一切ないか
- [ ] 1体のみか
