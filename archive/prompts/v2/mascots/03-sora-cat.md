# Subject
A single slim, elegant cat with a long tail, drawn in a quiet, mature
editorial illustration style. NOT a cartoon mascot. NOT a kitten.
NOT a predatory hunter pose.

The cat is in mid-walk pose, captured in a calm step. One front paw is
lifted slightly, the long tail curves softly behind, lifted to the upper
right of the frame. The head is turned 3/4 toward the viewer, looking
back over the shoulder as if to say "follow if you like" — but without
demanding.

The eyes are **narrow, half-closed**, calm — like an adult cat on
a quiet morning walk. NOT wide alert eyes. NOT manga-cat-girl eyes.
The mouth is a simple small line, closed.

# Composition
- Single cat in profile-to-3/4 angle, body facing right, head turned back
  to viewer.
- Character occupies about 60% of the canvas width.
- Long tail extends into the upper-right area, curving softly.
- A few very faint watercolor leaf marks on the implied ground beneath,
  ghostly and minimal.
- Background: transparent if possible, otherwise soft cream wash with
  hints of moss-green at the edges.

# Lighting
- Soft, dappled morning light (as if walking through tree shade).
- Warm honey-gold highlights on the back and tail tip.
- No harsh shadows, no strong contrast.

# Color palette
- Body: muted moss-cream (#D4D9C0, #B5BCA0) — slightly cooler than warm cream
- Belly/chest: paler cream (#EFEBE0)
- Tail tip and inner ears: slightly darker moss (#9BA481)
- Eye color: warm amber (#A88654), narrow and half-closed
- Nose: dusty rose (#B89888)
- Outline: thin sepia ink (#7E5A40, ~1.5pt)
- Leaf marks (if any): very low-opacity moss (#A2AB7D at 30%)
- Background: cream paper (#FAF6EE) if not transparent

# Texture
- Watercolor paper grain in the background.
- Hand-drawn sepia ink outline, gently confident.
- Soft fur indicated by subtle directional brush marks.
- A few faint watercolor splashes for atmosphere (very restrained).

# Mood
- **Quietly curious, calmly inviting, independent**.
- "Come walk with me. Or don't. Either is fine."
- The kind of cat that you might glimpse passing through an old Kyoto garden
  at dawn — present but not demanding attention.
- NOT playful, NOT pouncing, NOT alert hunting mode.

# Negative space
- 20%+ margins.
- Right-upper space accommodates the curving tail.
- Lower space is left mostly empty (the cat appears to be walking forward).

# Style references
- MUJI catalog illustrations
- **Komako Sakai**'s gentle cat sketches
- Studio Ghibli quieter cat moments (NOT Cat Bus, NOT Baron's flashy side)
- Edward Gorey's gentler animal sketches
- Yumi Kitagishi
- AVOID: anime cat girls, Sanrio Hello Kitty, manga cute cats,
  Garfield-style cartoons, photorealistic cat photography, Pixar cats

# Camera / Perspective
- Profile-to-3/4 angle.
- Camera at the cat's eye level (low, near the ground).
- Walking direction: from left to right, head turned back.
- Lens: 50mm natural, slight depth of field.

# Avoid
- Wide alert eyes, predatory pose, pouncing stance.
- Big anime sparkle eyes.
- Clothing, accessories, decorations (no collars, no bows, no bells).
- Any human gestures or expressions on the cat's face.
- Saturated colors, pure white background, pure black ink.
- Multiple cats. ONLY ONE.
- Photorealism, 3D rendering.
- Medical, clinical, hospital imagery.

- **半目・つっけんどん**が核。猫らしさを残しつつ、媚びない。

- 装飾品（首輪・リボン・鈴）は禁止。
