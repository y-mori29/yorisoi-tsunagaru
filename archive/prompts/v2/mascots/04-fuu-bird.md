# Subject
A single small, plump, round bird (sparrow-like or finch-like, generic
small songbird — NOT a specific dramatic species), drawn in a quiet,
mature editorial illustration style.

The bird is in mid-hover pose with wings gently spread, carrying a small
folded letter (cream paper) in its beak. The body is rounded, the tail
short and softly fanned. Legs are tucked under, barely visible.

The eyes are **tiny ink dots** with a calm, focused expression — NOT
alert hunter eyes. The beak holds the letter delicately, not aggressively.

# Composition
- Single bird in mid-flight, centered.
- Bird occupies about 50% of the canvas (smaller than other mascots,
  because it is small).
- Two or three tiny floating cream paper rectangles around it, as if
  scattered by its flight — very subtle, low opacity.
- Wings are spread but in a gentle position (NOT dramatic flapping).
- Background: transparent if possible, otherwise soft cream wash with
  hints of dusty purple at the upper edge.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused light from above.
- Warm highlights on the top of the head and back.
- Wing feathers slightly translucent where they catch the light.

# Color palette
- Body: muted dusty lavender-gray (#C8BFC8, #A89BA8) — distinctly different
  from the other mascots
- Wing tips and tail: slightly darker plum-gray (#8E7E92)
- Chest: paler cream-lavender (#E8E0E8)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Beak: pale dusty rose (#B8907D)
- Letter in beak: cream (#FAF6EE), tied with a thin moss-green thread (#A2AB7D)
- Outline: thin sepia ink (#7E5A40, ~1.5pt)
- Background: cream paper (#FAF6EE) if not transparent

# Texture
- Watercolor paper grain in the background.
- Hand-drawn sepia ink outlines.
- Feathers indicated by subtle, slightly varied line marks (NOT photoreal
  feather rendering).
- A few faint floating cream paper rectangles for atmosphere.

# Mood
- **Light, gentle, gracefully focused, never urgent**.
- "Here is a small letter for you. Take your time reading it."
- Like a small messenger from a quiet old village — present but not
  demanding.

# Negative space
- 20%+ margins.
- The floating paper rectangles around the bird occupy negative space
  without crowding.

# Style references
- MUJI catalog illustrations
- **Yumi Kitagishi**'s quiet bird studies
- Beatrix Potter's bird sketches (the gentlest, most restrained ones)
- Studio Ghibli small messenger birds (calm moments)
- Japanese sumi-e style restraint
- AVOID: anime style, cute Sanrio bird mascots, Disney bluebirds,
  realistic 3D bird, raptor/predator imagery, neon-colored tropical birds

# Camera / Perspective
- 3/4 angle, bird facing the viewer at a slight side angle.
- Eye-level with the bird (mid-air, camera floating).
- Lens: 50mm natural perspective.

# Avoid
- Sharp beak emphasized, predatory eyes, raptor pose, hawk silhouette.
- Big anime sparkle eyes.
- Clothing, accessories, decorations (no scarves, no hats — just the letter).
- Realistic photographic bird textures.
- Saturated colors, pure white background, pure black ink.
- Multiple birds. ONLY ONE.
- Letters with visible writing/text on them.
- Any medical or hospital imagery.
