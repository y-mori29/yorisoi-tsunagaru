# Subject
A quiet afternoon still life on a wooden table. The scene includes:
- a small ceramic plate with a single piece of fruit (a fig, or a half-cut
  pear), placed off-center
- an open notebook with blank cream pages (no readable text), spine facing left
- a small linen cloth folded next to it
- a single dried flower stem or pressed leaf placed on the open page

No people. No medical references. No phone or modern devices.

Soft, midday-afternoon window light from the side. The whole scene is
intentionally quiet and slightly nostalgic.

# Composition
- Square 1:1 aspect ratio.
- Wooden table fills the lower 70% of the frame.
- Plate with fruit at lower-left third.
- Notebook in the right-center.
- Linen cloth tucked under the notebook.
- Upper 30% is soft warm wall in the background (out of focus).

# Lighting
- Warm afternoon light from the upper-left (window light).
- Soft shadows that fall to the right at gentle angles.
- No harsh highlights. Everything is gently illuminated.

# Color palette
- Wood table: warm honey-walnut (#A47556, #7E5A40)
- Ceramic plate: cream with muted terracotta rim (#EFDCC9 / #C99F7B)
- Fruit (fig/pear): muted plum-rose (#A07866) or pale moss-yellow (#B5B584)
- Notebook pages: cream-white (#FAF6EE)
- Notebook cover: warm beige cloth (#D6C8B2)
- Linen cloth: pale cream (#EFDCC9)
- Dried flower/leaf: muted moss (#A2AB7D) or sepia (#7E5A40)
- Background wall: soft cream beige (#F3EDE2)

# Texture
- Visible wood grain on the table, slightly worn.
- Soft fabric texture on the linen.
- Hand-thrown ceramic feel on the plate (slight imperfections).
- Paper grain visible on the notebook pages.
- Photographic-illustration quality: realistic but slightly painterly.

# Mood
- **Slow afternoon. Time stops. Permission to pause.**
- Like a still moment from a Naomi Kawase film.
- Magazine layout reminiscent of Kinfolk or Brutus's Japan specials.

# Negative space
- Upper 30% is mostly negative space (wall + light).
- Right edge has breathing room.

# Style references
- Kinfolk magazine layouts
- Hiroshi Sugimoto's quiet stillness
- Naoya Hatakeyama photography
- Hayao Miyazaki's slow domestic scenes (no characters, just the room)
- AVOID: food blog photography, Instagram coffee-shop aesthetic,
  stock photography, high-saturation product shots

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the table surface (camera at table height).
- Slight 3/4 angle from upper-right.
- Lens: 50mm natural, shallow depth of field.

# Avoid
- Any people, faces, hands, or human silhouettes.
- Modern objects: phones, laptops, branded items.
- Medical items: bottles, syringes, pill containers.
- Saturated colors, bright pinks, neon highlights.
- Children-book style, cartoon style.
- Text on the notebook (must be blank or illegible).
- Steam, smoke, dramatic light rays.
- Multiple objects of the same type (one fruit, one notebook, one cloth).
