# Subject
A small folded cream-paper letter resting on a worn wooden surface (an
old table or windowsill). Beside the letter:
- a small dried flower stem laid across the letter, casual but
  intentional
- a wax-sealed envelope flap visible (the seal in deep terracotta wax,
  unstamped, abstract)
- a single thin ribbon of muted moss-green silk

No people. No hands. No clear text on the letter (must be illegible or
just abstract pen marks).

This is the kind of letter that says: "Someone, somewhere, thought of you."

# Composition
- Square 1:1.
- The letter (folded once) takes center-stage.
- The dried flower stem lies diagonally across the letter (lower-left to
  upper-right).
- The silk ribbon trails to the right edge.
- Wax seal visible in the upper-left corner of the letter.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused window light from upper-left.
- Gentle shadow falls to the right of the letter.
- No harsh highlights.

# Color palette
- Wooden surface: warm honey walnut (#7E5A40, with visible grain)
- Letter paper: cream (#FAF6EE, slightly aged with subtle yellowing)
- Wax seal: deep muted terracotta (#A47556)
- Dried flower: faded moss-sage (#A2AB7D) with slight terracotta tinge
- Silk ribbon: muted moss-green (#9BA481)
- Background (out of focus area): warm cream shadow (#D6C8B2)

# Texture
- Visible wood grain on the surface.
- Paper grain on the letter, slightly aged.
- The wax seal has the texture of dripped, slightly cracked wax.
- The dried flower has a brittle, dried texture.
- The silk ribbon has a soft satin sheen.

# Mood
- **Quiet anticipation. Care without expectation.**
- Like opening an unexpected handwritten note from a friend you haven't
  seen in years.
- Slow, deliberate, warm.

# Negative space
- The upper-right and lower edges have generous wooden surface space.
- The composition should feel deliberate but not crowded.

# Style references
- Vermeer's quiet still lifes
- Naoya Hatakeyama photography
- Japanese tea ceremony aesthetics (静けさ・侘び)
- AVOID: vintage stock images, Pinterest "snail mail" aesthetic,
  scrapbooking photography, overly decorative calligraphy

# Camera / Perspective
- Overhead shot (camera looking straight down at the table).
- Slight tilt (a few degrees) for natural feel.
- Lens: 50mm, very shallow depth of field on the letter.

# Avoid
- Any visible writing or readable text on the letter (must be illegible
  pen marks at most, or completely blank surface).
- Modern stationery branding.
- Postage stamps, addresses, ZIP codes.
- People or hands holding the letter.
- Multiple letters (only one).
- Saturated colors.
- Photorealistic high-contrast detail.
- Heart shapes, romantic decoration.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: 「そっと届く声」「お知らせ」画面のヘッダー、または通知が届いたときの装飾。「誰かが、あなたを思った」を視覚化する。

**ポイント**:
- 文字が読める形で写ってはいけない（読めるとリアルになりすぎる）。
- 派手な色のリボンや装飾物は禁止。
- 「ロマンチック」になりすぎないように（恋文ではなく、友人からの便り）。

**チェック**:
- [ ] 封筒や紙面に読める文字がないか
- [ ] 配色がアースカラーでまとまっているか
- [ ] ハート・スタンプなどがないか
- [ ] 人物・手が写っていないか
