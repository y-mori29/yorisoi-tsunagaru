# Subject
A small ceramic cup placed on a wooden surface, half-hidden behind a thin
sheer linen curtain that drapes softly across part of the frame.
The cup is visible but partially obscured by the curtain's translucency.
A single dried flower stem peeks out from behind the curtain.

The composition suggests: "What you're looking for might just be on the
other side."

# Composition
- Square 1:1.
- Cup positioned at center-right.
- Sheer linen curtain drapes from upper-right to lower-left, covering
  about 40% of the frame at varying translucency.
- Dried flower stem visible at the curtain's lower edge.
- Wooden surface visible in the bottom third.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused window light from behind the curtain.
- Gentle silhouette effect through the curtain.

# Color palette
- Wood surface: warm honey (#A47556)
- Cup: muted cream-terracotta (#C99F7B)
- Sheer linen curtain: pale cream (#F3EDE2) with subtle warm tint
- Dried flower: muted moss-sage (#A2AB7D)
- Light from behind: warm cream (#FAF6EE)

# Texture
- Sheer linen weave visible in the curtain.
- Wood grain on the surface.
- Subtle watercolor wash overall.

# Mood
- **Not found, but not gone forever. The veil might lift.**
- Patient mystery. Hope without urgency.
- Like a sliding paper door (障子) at a quiet inn.

# Negative space
- The curtain's translucent area is intentional soft space.
- Generous wood surface in the lower portion.

# Style references
- Japanese sliding door (shōji) aesthetics
- Naoya Hatakeyama photography
- AVOID: cartoon magnifying glass with sad face, "404 page" tropes,
  detective/mystery genre imagery

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the surface.
- Slight 3/4 angle.

# Avoid
- Magnifying glasses, question marks, "?" symbols.
- Characters or mascots.
- Dark or noir aesthetics.
- Saturated colors.
- Text overlays.
