# Subject
A small ceramic cup placed on a wooden tabletop, alone. The cup is empty.
A single dried leaf rests beside it. Soft window light from above
illuminates the scene. The composition is intentionally minimal and quiet.

This image says: "Nothing has happened yet. That's okay."

# Composition
- Square 1:1.
- Cup and leaf in the lower-center, occupying about 40% of the canvas.
- Generous empty wooden table around them.
- Upper 50% is soft light fading into cream wall.

# Lighting
- Soft window light from above-left.
- Gentle shadow under the cup.

# Color palette
- Wood table: warm honey (#A47556)
- Cup: cream ceramic with terracotta rim (#EFDCC9 / #C99F7B)
- Leaf: muted moss-sage (#A2AB7D)
- Background light: warm cream (#FAF6EE)

# Texture
- Wood grain visible on table.
- Hand-thrown ceramic feel on cup.
- Brittle dried leaf texture.
- Subtle watercolor wash on background.

# Mood
- **Patient emptiness. Quiet beginning. Permission to wait.**
- Not "lonely." Not "sad." Just "quiet."
- Like the moment before a kettle starts to whistle.

# Negative space
- 60%+ of the canvas is intentional negative space (table + wall).

# Style references
- MUJI catalog still life
- Kinfolk magazine
- AVOID: sad-faced illustrations, "empty inbox" tropes, cartoon ghosts/zzz,
  any illustration with a melancholy character

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the table.
- Slight 3/4 angle.

# Avoid
- Any characters (no animals, no people, no mascots).
- "Nothing here" text overlays.
- Sad imagery, crying drops, dark colors.
- Saturated colors.
- Multiple cups or items.
