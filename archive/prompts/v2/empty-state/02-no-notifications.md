# Subject
A wooden window sill with a small empty letter tray (or a small wooden
box) placed on it. The tray is empty. A single sprig of dried lavender
rests beside it. The window beyond is softly out of focus, suggesting
a quiet morning outside.

This image says: "Nothing urgent today. Rest."

# Composition
- Square 1:1.
- Window sill takes the lower 60% of the frame.
- Empty letter tray (or wooden box) in the center.
- Lavender sprig to the right of the tray.
- Upper 40%: out-of-focus window light, faint cream curtain edge.

# Lighting
- Soft morning light from the window (upper-right).
- Gentle shadows under the tray.

# Color palette
- Wood sill: warm honey (#7E5A40)
- Letter tray: muted cream wood (#D6C8B2)
- Lavender: muted plum-gray (#A89BA8) — NOT saturated purple
- Window light: warm cream (#F3EDE2)
- Background (window): pale cream-peach with faint hints of trees outside

# Texture
- Visible wood grain.
- Soft fabric texture on the curtain (out of focus).
- Dried lavender texture (brittle, faded).

# Mood
- **No urgency today. Permission to slow down.**
- Like a quiet Sunday morning with no obligations.

# Negative space
- Generous empty sill space.
- Out-of-focus background creates more negative space.

# Style references
- MUJI catalog
- Kinfolk slow living layouts
- AVOID: cartoon "no mail" tropes, sad envelope illustrations,
  empty mailbox with cobwebs imagery

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the sill.
- Slight 3/4 angle from the right.

# Avoid
- Cobwebs, dust piles (suggesting "abandoned").
- Mail icons, envelope cartoons.
- Saturated lavender (must be muted gray-purple).
- Characters or mascots.
- Text overlays.
