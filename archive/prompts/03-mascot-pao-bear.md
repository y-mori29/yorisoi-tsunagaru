# Subject
A single, soft, round, very fluffy bear character named "Pao."
He is sitting on his bottom, both back legs comfortably stretched out forward,
his round belly resting softly. His arms are at his sides, slightly resting
on his lap. His head is slightly tilted, looking calmly and a little drowsily
toward the viewer.

Pao is not a "tough" bear at all — he is a soft, cuddly, plushie-like
presence. His ears are small and rounded, his snout is short, his nose is
a tiny soft dot. His eyes are tiny soft dots (NOT anime-style).

His fur color is a warm cream-beige (#F0DCC2 → #D9C19E gradient), with
slightly darker accents around his snout and inner ears. He has a small
piece of clover or a tiny dried flower attached to his chest, like a quiet
brooch.

This character represents "the quiet friend who just sits next to you
without saying anything, and somehow that's enough."

# Composition
- Centered single character, full body visible, sitting pose.
- Character occupies the central 65% of the canvas.
- Background: ideally pure transparent — fallback to soft cream wash
  (#FFFBF5) with subtle watercolor edges.
- No floor, no furniture, no setting — just floating soft form.

# Lighting
- Soft diffused light from upper-left.
- Warm honey-gold highlights on the top of the head and shoulders.
- Very gentle drop-shadow under the body to suggest weight.

# Color palette
- Body: warm cream-beige (#F0DCC2, #D9C19E)
- Snout area: slightly darker cream (#C9AC85)
- Inner ears: soft peach pink (#F2CFB8)
- Clover/flower accent: matcha green (#C7DBA8) or soft peach (#FFD9C7)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Tiny nose: dusty rose (#A87463)

# Texture
- Visible watercolor paper grain in the background wash.
- Soft fur indicated by very fine, gentle line marks.
- Slightly imperfect, hand-drawn ink outlines in warm brown (#6B4F3F).
- "Plushie" softness — looks like he would be very warm to hug.

# Mood
- Sleepy, calm, comforting, unconditionally present.
- "I'm here. I won't go anywhere."
- The kind of bear that doesn't need to do anything to make you feel safer.
- Reminiscent of the gentle warmth of Hayao Miyazaki's My Neighbor Totoro
  (especially the small Totoro), and Beatrix Potter's softer bear sketches.

# Negative space
- Plenty of margin around the character (15-20% on all sides) for flexible
  cropping in the app UI.

# Style references
- Studio Ghibli (My Neighbor Totoro, especially the small white/grey Totoro
  but warmer in color)
- Picture book art: Komako Sakai, Yoshitake Shinsuke, Hiroshi Saito
- Old Pooh Bear illustrations by E.H. Shepard (gentle pencil sketch warmth)
- AVOID: anime style, manga, vector design, corporate mascot,
  realistic 3D bear, scary or growling bear.

# Camera / Perspective
- Slight low angle (camera at the bear's belly level looking slightly up).
- Frontal, very slight 10-degree tilt to one side for a relaxed feel.

# Avoid
- Big anime eyes, shiny highlights, glittery effects.
- Any aggressive features (sharp teeth, claws emphasized, growling).
- Any human clothing, human gestures (thumbs up, peace signs).
- Any medical, hospital, clinical iconography.
- Text, logos, watermarks.
- Photorealism, hyperdetail.
- Multiple characters — ONLY ONE bear in the frame.
- Sexualization of any kind.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: 静かに隣にいるくま「ぱお」。空状態（投稿ゼロ・通知ゼロ）の画面で「ここにいるよ」と無言で寄り添う役。多くを語らない安心感。

**ポイント**:
- 「**眠そう・くつろいでる**」が核。元気いっぱいではない。
- 立ち姿ではなく **座り姿**（足を投げ出してくつろぐ感じ）。
- 色は **クリームベージュ**。茶色いリアルなクマにしない（重くなる）。
- プッシー（ぬいぐるみ）っぽい質感を狙う。

**生成後にチェックしたい点**:
- [ ] 強そうな顔・牙・爪が描かれていないか
- [ ] 表情が「眠そう/穏やか」で「ニコニコ」になっていないか
- [ ] 1体のみか
- [ ] ぬいぐるみのような柔らかさが出ているか

**失敗時の修正方針**:
- 強い顔になった → "soft, plush, cuddly, no aggression, sleepy expression" を強調
- 立ち姿になった → "Sitting pose, legs stretched forward, NOT standing" を強調
