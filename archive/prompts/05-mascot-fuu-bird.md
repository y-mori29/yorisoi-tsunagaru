# Subject
A single, very small, round, fluffy bird character named "Fuu."
She is mid-flight in a gentle hover, wings spread wide and softly,
carrying a tiny folded piece of paper (a letter) in her beak.
Her body is round and plump, her tail short and rounded, her legs
tiny tucked under her. Her eyes are small soft dots with a gentle
expression — NOT alert hunter eyes.

Fuu's plumage is a soft lavender-grey color (#E0D6E8 → #B8A8C9 gradient),
with slightly darker lavender on her wing tips and tail. Her chest is
a paler cream-lavender. The tiny letter in her beak is folded cream paper,
tied with a thin green thread (matcha).

This character represents "the messenger who carries soft news — never
urgent, never demanding, just gentle delivery."

# Composition
- Centered single character, mid-hover pose, wings spread.
- Character occupies the central 50-60% of the canvas (slightly smaller
  than other mascots because she's a small bird).
- Two or three tiny floating cream paper letters/petals around her,
  as if scattered by her flight (very soft, low opacity).
- Background: pure transparent ideally, or very soft cream wash with
  faint lavender tint at the edges.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused light from above.
- Warm highlights on the top of the head and back.
- Wing feathers slightly translucent where they catch the light.

# Color palette
- Body: soft lavender-grey (#E0D6E8, #B8A8C9)
- Wing tips and tail: slightly darker lavender (#8E7AA0)
- Chest: pale cream-lavender (#F5EFFA)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Beak: pale dusty rose (#D9B0A0)
- Letter in beak: cream (#FFFBF5) with matcha thread (#9BB87A)

# Texture
- Watercolor paper grain in the background.
- Fine pencil/ink outlines in warm brown (#6B4F3F).
- Feathers indicated by gentle, slightly varied line marks (not photoreal).
- A few faint sparkle dots in the air for atmosphere.

# Mood
- Light, gentle, gracefully busy in a quiet way.
- "Here's a small letter for you. Take your time reading it."
- The kind of bird that brings news without urgency.
- Reminiscent of small messenger birds from Studio Ghibli films,
  and Beatrix Potter's gentle bird sketches.

# Negative space
- Plenty of room around the bird so the small floating papers/petals
  can be visible without crowding.
- Margins 15-20%.

# Style references
- Studio Ghibli small birds
- Picture book illustrations: Beatrix Potter's bird studies (gentlest ones),
  Yoshitake Shinsuke
- AVOID: anime style, manga, vector flat design, corporate logo bird,
  realistic 3D bird, predator/raptor poses

# Camera / Perspective
- Slight side view, bird facing the viewer at 3/4 angle.
- Eye-level with the bird, mid-air.

# Avoid
- Sharp beak emphasized, predatory eyes, raptor pose.
- Big anime eyes, sparkly highlights.
- Any clothing or accessories beyond the letter.
- Realistic photographic bird textures.
- Any medical or hospital imagery.
- Text, logos, watermarks (the letter in beak should NOT have visible writing).
- Photorealism, 3D rendering.
- Multiple birds — ONLY ONE bird.
- Sexualization of any kind.
