# Subject
A single small rabbit, drawn in a quiet, mature, editorial illustration style.
NOT a children's book character. NOT a cartoon mascot. NOT kawaii.

The rabbit is sitting calmly in a slightly turned 3/4 pose, facing the viewer
but slightly to the side. Its posture is composed and quiet — like a small
animal in a Japanese tea garden at dawn. The expression is **almost
expressionless**: tiny ink-dot eyes, a soft small mouth that is essentially
closed. No smile. No big eyes. No exaggerated cuteness.

The rabbit feels like a respectful, quiet presence — the kind you might see
in a literary magazine illustration or on the cover of a thoughtful Japanese
essay collection. Think MUJI catalog aesthetic, or a quiet moment from a
Studio Ghibli film (but more restrained than Totoro — closer to the small
forest animals in Princess Mononoke's quieter scenes).

# Composition
- Single rabbit, centered, occupying about 55-60% of the canvas height.
- Plenty of breathing room: at least 20% margin on all sides.
- No background scenery. No floor. No props.
- Background: soft cream-paper wash (#FAF6EE) with subtle watercolor edges,
  OR ideally transparent PNG.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused, single light source from upper left.
- No drama. No strong shadows.
- Faint, almost imperceptible shadow under the body to suggest grounding.

# Color palette
- Body: warm cream-beige (#EFDCC9, #E0C2A5) — muted, slightly grayed
- Inner ears: pale dusty rose (#E0C2A5 with slight pink tint, NOT bright pink)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A)
- Outline: thin, hand-drawn, warm sepia ink (#7E5A40, line weight ~1.5pt)
- Background (if not transparent): cream paper (#FAF6EE)
- **Avoid**: bright pink, saturated pastels, pure white, pure black

# Texture
- Fine watercolor paper grain visible in subtle areas.
- Hand-drawn ink outline: slightly imperfect, like a single confident pen
  stroke (not retraced).
- Soft watercolor wash inside the outline — minimal layering.
- A few delicate dry-brush marks for fur texture, very restrained.

# Mood
- **Quiet, composed, patient, respectful**.
- The kind of presence that makes you feel "It's okay to be quiet."
- Not heartwarming in a cloying way. Heartwarming in a dignified way.
- Like meeting a small wild animal who has decided to sit near you — neither
  performing for you nor avoiding you.

# Negative space
- Generous margins all around (20%+).
- Designed so the rabbit can be placed inside a small UI circle (avatar)
  AND blown up large as a hero image.

# Style references
- MUJI Japanese catalog illustrations
- Editorial illustrations in literary magazines (Bungeishunju, Kurashi-no-Techo)
- Hayao Miyazaki's restrained background animal sketches
- Yumi Kitagishi's quiet animal illustrations
- Quentin Blake at his most restrained
- AVOID: Sanrio, anime, Pixar 3D, Disney cute, Beatrix Potter (too sweet),
  Western greeting card art

# Camera / Perspective
- Eye-level with the rabbit (camera low to the ground).
- Slight 3/4 angle, mostly frontal.
- Lens equivalent: 50mm natural perspective.

# Avoid
- **Big anime eyes**, sparkle highlights, exaggerated cuteness.
- Any cartoon characteristics (big head, tiny body proportions, neon outlines).
- Any clothing, accessories, props (no leaves in ears, no flowers, no hats).
- Any human gestures (waving paws, peace signs, hugs).
- Smiling broadly. Open mouth. Tongue out.
- Saturated pastels (pink, mint, lavender at high saturation).
- Pure white background. Pure black ink.
- Children-book illustration style.
- Multiple rabbits. Only ONE.
- Any medical, clinical, or hospital iconography.
- Text, logos, watermarks of any kind.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: v1のもかは「絵本の主人公」になっていて子供っぽさが出てしまった。v2では「ふと部屋の隅にいる小さなうさぎ」のように、**主張せず・媚びず・静かに居る**存在感を目指す。

**ポイント**:
- 目は **小さな黒い点だけ**。アニメ目・キラキラ目は絶対NG。
- 口は **ほぼ閉じている**。微笑みすらしない。
- ポーズは **完全な静止**。ジェスチャー・動きを匂わせない。
- 色は **くすみテラコッタ系のベージュ**。ピンクや原色のパステルは絶対NG。
- スタイルは **MUJIカタログ / 文芸誌の挿絵** のような大人寄り。
- v1の「ふわふわ・もこもこ」表現は廃止。ライン画ベース + 控えめな水彩。

**生成後にチェックしたい点**:
- [ ] 目が点で表現されているか
- [ ] 口が閉じられているか・笑っていないか
- [ ] 色がくすんで大人っぽいか（鮮やかすぎないか）
- [ ] ポーズに動きがなく静止しているか
- [ ] 装飾（葉っぱ・花・帽子）が混入していないか
- [ ] 絵本キャラっぽくないか・編集系のイラストに近いか
