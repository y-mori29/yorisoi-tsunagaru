# Subject
A single, soft, round, slightly chubby rabbit character named "Moka."
She is sitting calmly on her hind legs, facing slightly to the viewer's right
at a 3/4 angle. Her front paws are gently folded in front of her chest, as if
she is patiently listening to someone. Her eyes are tiny soft dots (NOT
anime-style big eyes), her expression is gentle and slightly smiling.

She wears a small dried-leaf accessory on her left ear, like a tiny leaf
slipped into her hair. No clothing, no human features beyond the listening
posture.

Moka's fur color is a very pale peach-cream (#FFE3D2 → #FFCFB5 gradient),
with slightly darker cream around her ears and paws. Her inner ears are
the softest blush pink. She has a small, round, fluffy tail visible behind her.

This character represents "the friend who sits next to you and just listens,
without trying to fix anything."

# Composition
- Centered single character, full body visible, head-to-feet.
- Character occupies the central 60% of the canvas.
- Light vignette of soft cream around the character; no hard background.
- Background: ideally pure transparent (PNG with alpha) — but if transparency
  is not supported, use a very soft cream wash (#FFFBF5) with faint
  watercolor edges, NOT a hard color block.
- No floor, no furniture, no setting elements. Just the character floating
  on a soft wash.

# Lighting
- Soft, diffused light from upper-left, like morning sun through a curtain.
- Very gentle warm highlights on the top of the head and shoulders.
- No harsh shadows. Slight soft drop-shadow under the body to ground her.

# Color palette
- Body: pale peach-cream (#FFE3D2, #FFCFB5)
- Inner ears: blush pink (#FFC4B0)
- Leaf accessory: matcha green (#C7DBA8)
- Eye dots: warm dark brown (#3A2E2A) — NOT pure black
- Cheek blush: very faint peach (#FFB89A at 30% opacity)
- Background wash (if used): cream #FFFBF5

# Texture
- Visible watercolor paper grain, especially in the background wash.
- Soft pigment bleeds at fur edges.
- Slightly imperfect, slightly trembling hand-drawn ink outlines (thin, brown #6B4F3F).
- Fur has a "fluffy" texture indicated by tiny line marks, not photorealistic.

# Mood
- Calm, gentle, patient, slightly melancholy but warm.
- "I am here. You don't need to say anything."
- The kind of presence that makes you exhale.
- Reminiscent of Hayao Miyazaki's small forest creatures and
  Yoshitake Shinsuke's gentle picture book characters.

# Negative space
- Generous space around the character so she can be cropped/placed flexibly.
- Allow at least 15% margin on all sides.

# Style references
- Studio Ghibli small forest spirits (especially from Princess Mononoke's
  Kodama, but warmer)
- Picture book illustrations: Yoshitake Shinsuke, Beatrix Potter's gentlest
  rabbit drawings, Kazuo Iwamura
- AVOID: anime character art, manga eyes, vector flat design, corporate
  mascot style, Sanrio-style high contrast cuteness

# Camera / Perspective
- Front-facing, slightly 3/4 angle (rotated about 15 degrees so we can see
  some of her side).
- Eye-level with the character (camera at her height).

# Avoid
- Big anime eyes or shiny highlight reflections in eyes.
- Any human clothing or human posture beyond "sitting and listening."
- Any medical equipment, hospital setting, white coats, doctors, nurses.
- Any text, logos, watermarks, signatures, character names written.
- Photorealism, 3D rendering, hyperdetailed fur.
- Aggressive or "uplifting" expressions (no big smiles, no thumbs up).
- Sexualization of any kind.
- Sharp geometric shapes or neon colors.
- Multiple characters — ONLY ONE rabbit in the frame.

# 日本語End note

**意図**: 「よりそい つながる」の公式メインマスコット「もか」。聞き役・案内役・空状態の癒し役として全画面で登場する核キャラ。

**ポイント**:
- 「**聞き役**」感を出すために、口は閉じ気味、目は静か、姿勢は前傾でなく安定。
- 「治してあげる」「励ましてあげる」感のあるサムズアップやガッツポーズは禁止。
- 子供向けっぽくなりすぎないように、サンリオ的なツヤツヤしたかわいさは避ける（絵本のあたたかさへ）。
- 配色はピーチクリームで固定。茶色や灰色はNG（重くなる）。

**生成後にチェックしたい点**:
- [ ] 目が大きすぎないか（小さい点のような目になっているか）
- [ ] 表情が「微笑」程度で「満面の笑み」になっていないか
- [ ] 医療っぽい要素が混入していないか
- [ ] 一体のみで描かれているか（複数体になっていないか）
- [ ] 背景が透明 or 柔らかいクリームウォッシュになっているか（ベタ塗り背景ではないか）

**失敗時の修正方針**:
- アニメ風になった → Style references に "AVOID anime / manga" を強調、Subject に "NOT anime style. Picture-book illustration style." を冒頭追加
- 目が大きい → "Tiny dot eyes, NOT big eyes" をSubjectに追加
- 複数体になった → "ONLY ONE rabbit in the entire image" をCompositionに追加
